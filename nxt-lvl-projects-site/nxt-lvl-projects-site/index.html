Why it matters

Your next customer is looking for you right now.

Before people call, visit or buy, they check you out on their phone. What they find in those few seconds decides whether they choose you or the business down the road.

01

They search before they buy.

Customers look you up, compare you and read your reviews, often late at night on their sofa. If you're hard to find, they never get as far as calling.

02

First impressions are online.

A slow, dated or missing website makes a great business look smaller than it is. A sharp one makes you look like the obvious choice.

03

Easy wins.

When booking, ordering or getting a quote takes one tap, people do it there and then, instead of "ringing tomorrow" and forgetting.

    FAQ

    Questions people ask.

    Is the draft really free?

    Yes. I build a working draft of your site so you can see it on your own phone before deciding anything. If you don't want to go ahead, you owe nothing.

    I'm not a salon or a café. Will it work for my business?

    Yes. I build for all kinds of small businesses: trades, shops, producers, tourism, health and fitness, professional services and more. Every site is designed around what your customers need to do, whether that's booking, ordering, calling or asking for a quote.

    How much does a website cost?

    Sites start from €500 for a one-page site, €1,000 for a full business site and €1,500 for bigger projects, with care plans from €39 a month. You'll get a fixed price after our first chat, and I'll tell you whether the LEO voucher could cover part of it.

    Do we need to meet in person?

    No. Everything is done by phone, video call and email, so I work with businesses anywhere in Ireland.

    I already have a Facebook page. Isn't that enough?

    Facebook is great for updates, but people searching Google for a business like yours often won't find it, you don't own it, and it can't take bookings, orders or quotes the way a proper site can. Your website and your social pages work best together.

    Can it work with my booking or ordering system?

    Usually, yes. Fresha, Phorest, Booksy, Timely, restaurant booking tools, Shopify and most other systems can be linked from your site, so customers use the system you already have.

    Will I be able to update it myself?

    Yes. I'll show you how to change prices, hours and photos. If you'd rather not, updates can be part of a care plan.

    How long does it take?

    Your free draft usually comes first, then most sites go live within a few weeks, depending on how quickly we get your photos, words and feedback.
